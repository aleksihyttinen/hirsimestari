# Hirsimestari – notes for Claude Code

Single-player, Finnish-language log-building game (PWA) for people with intellectual disabilities. The player picks a building, places pieces (tap-then-tap or drag), optionally paints it (houses only), and everything is saved. No levels, timers, penalties or failure screens. Goal: the feeling of finishing something, not learning.

## Commands
- `npm run dev` – dev server (no service worker here)
- `npm test` – vitest, pure game logic only (`src/game/game.test.ts`)
- `npm run typecheck` – `tsc --noEmit`, strict
- `npm run build` then `npm run preview` – the only way to check PWA/offline behaviour

Run `typecheck`, `test` and `build` before calling any change done.

## Stack (do not add to it without a clear reason)
React 18, strict TypeScript, Vite, SVG, plain CSS, `vite-plugin-pwa`, localStorage. No backend, accounts, analytics, state library, game engine or UI kit.

## Layout
- `src/game/` – rules and data, no React, no DOM. Keep it that way so it stays testable.
  - `types.ts` – `Building`, `Target`, `Project`, `SaveData`
  - `buildings.ts` – buildings as data (`BUILDINGS`), palette, labels
  - `rules.ts` – `available`, `openTargets`, `canPlace`, `nearestTarget` (snapping), `place`, `undo`, `paint`, tray and hint (`trayKinds`, `pickKind`, `hintFor`)
  - `storage.ts` – load/save; never throws, drops unknown or malformed data
  - `plan.ts` – Sisusta: `whyNotFit`, `fitNear`, `turnNear`, `usedCells`, `left`, `recognized` (room recipes), `missing`, `cleanFurniture`
  - `gesture.ts` – tap vs drag threshold
- `src/Scene.tsx` – SVG drawing only (viewBox `0 0 400 300`; a floor plan is cropped to its grid). No game rules here.
- `src/App.tsx` – all screens (home, modes, pick, new, settings, game, carve, fix, help) and interaction.

## How the game works
- A `Target` is a slot (`x,y,w,h` in SVG units) with `after: string[]` prerequisites. Only targets whose prerequisites are placed are available, so the build order is data: logs bottom-up, then door and windows, then pier or porch and roof, then chimney or fire.
- Only available slots are drawn. The active one is yellow with a ＋ mark. With a single available piece kind it is auto-selected. A chosen piece stays chosen after placing (and after Peru) until none of it is left.
- Placing the last piece switches `phase` to `'paint'` (`finished` in `rules.ts`). "Valmis" sets `'done'`. A done house reopens in paint mode (derived in `App`, not saved). Hirsityöt is woodwork only: a frame goes straight to `'done'` and is never painted.
- A `Project` is one saved building: `{id, buildingId, placed[], colors{}, phase}`. Several per building are allowed.
- Hirsityöt (`Building.mode: 'runko'`; small Aitan runko on corner stones `stones: true`, medium Mökin runko, large Omakotitalon runko; the names follow the houses Laavu → Rantasauna → Mökki and the floor plan Omakotitalo, the ids `aitta`, `talorunko`, `pirtti` stay so saves still open): frames seen from the gable end, round pieces are log ends. Harder on purpose: every empty slot is shown, the tray offers all remaining kinds sorted by label A–Z, wall logs are their own kind `wall` ("Seinähirsi"), and wrong tries say why (`whyNot`, `KIND_INFO`). Its ? button opens the Rakennusohje guide (real terms). List a frame's two kengityshirret first (the kivijalka is drawn under them).
- Hirren veisto: when a frame is started the player chooses whether to hew (`Project.carve`). Targets with `carve: true` then open the hewing screen (`CARVE_STEPS`: kuorinta, veisto, vara, salvos) before placing; hewn ids go in `Project.carved`. Each step needs its tool (`TOOLS`, chosen below the log like every tray, reset every step); with the wrong tool the cut does not happen and a neutral hint says the tool does not fit (step hints never name the tool).
- Sisätyöt (`Building.mode: 'sisa'`; Keittiö, Kylpyhuone with `tiles: true`, Sauna (id `loylyhuone`) with a sähkökiuas and no water): rooms seen from inside, built in house order (only available slots shown). The shut-offs (`SHUTOFF`: sähkökeskus, then pääventtiili in wet rooms) are drawn on the wall from the start, are never in the tray, and are tapped directly; the lever shows I/0, the valve handle lies along or across the pipe. Once every piece is in, the same spots are tapped again to open the water and then turn the power back on (`on: true` targets added by `room()`, hints `SHUTON`); in a finished room a tap on them says they are already on and points to what to try. Every other kind is in `FIX` and opens the fastening screen before placing: pick the material, then the tool, then tap each spot. Each step offers the right item and two random others (`choices`), shuffled once per piece. `spots` sit where that fastener really goes (1–3 per piece). A wrong material says "<x> ei sovi <wrong>" and the reason (`MATERIALS.why`), a wrong tool names the material again; nothing changes. Fastening is not saved: an undone piece is fastened again. A finished room is `'done'`; switch, tap, shower and ohjauskeskus can be tried (`lit`, `TRY`, not saved; the ohjauskeskus runs the kiuas and the saunavalaisin).
- Sisusta (`Building.mode: 'pohja'`, `Building.plan`, empty `targets`; small to large like the other modes: Pihasauna 5×4 (pukuhuone with the outside door, löylyhuone with a fixed kiuas and vesipata), Mökki 8×6 (id `mokkipohja`: bedroom, pesuhuone, löylyhuone, one open room that is kitchen and living room, `Room.open`, with a pirtinpöytä and two benches as long as it, 2×1, and no coffee table or TV), Omakotitalo 10×7 (two bedrooms, a small hall, bathroom, kitchen, a four-row living room)): a floor plan from above on a grid of 40-unit cells (a cell can be under the 64 px rule on a landscape tablet; a drop only has to cover the cell). Every cell belongs to exactly one room, and washrooms and bathrooms are ready from the start (not in `goal`). Walls, doors (with swing), windows and fixtures (`plan.fixed`: stove, sink, WC, shower, basin, kiuas, vesipata) are drawn and never move; rooms are unnamed (`Room.name` is the screen-reader description). The player places furniture freely: drag it from the tray onto the plan (a dashed preview shows where it lands), or pick it and tap a cell (`plan.pieces` = the amounts a well-furnished house needs, plus a few free extras that belong to no recipe: wardrobes, chests of drawers, an armchair, plants; a chosen piece stays chosen while some are left); `fitNear` puts it anywhere that covers that cell. A piece stays inside one room, off fixtures, other pieces and the cells on both sides of a door; a refused tap says why, neutrally (`FIT_HINT`). Drag a placed piece to move it, or tap it to select it, then tap a new cell, Käännä (`turnNear`: a quarter turn, or a half turn when a quarter cannot fit), Poista or ✓ Valmis (ends the edit; the building's "<name> valmis" is hidden meanwhile so the two never mix). Peru undoes the last change (history in `App`, not saved). A room is recognised when one of its building's recipes holds (`plan.recipes`, data: `items` in the room, `beside` = a piece with N touching pieces, `entry` = the room with the outside door; the first one it meets, or all of them in an open room). Each building has its own rules: Omakotitalo bed + touching bedside table, table + two touching chairs with the stove, sofa + coffee table + TV, coat rack + shoe rack in the entry room; Mökki bed + bedside table, pirtinpöytä + two touching benches with the stove, sofa + armchair, lauteet + kiulu with the kiuas; Pihasauna bench + coat rack in the entry room, lauteet + kiulu with the kiuas. and gets a "✓ <name>" tag in its middle until the next move, or for 4 s and then fades (`.roomtag`; no fade with reduced motion), so tags never cover furniture for long; `plan.goal` lists the rooms a finished building has (Omakotitalo: two bedrooms) and `missing` drives the count; feedback is praise only, plus a tip (the missing room's `Recipe.text`) once every piece is out and a room is missing. "<name> valmis" sets `'done'`; reopening keeps editing. Saved in `Project.furniture`, cleaned on load (`cleanFurniture`).
- Ohje: every screen has a ? button (home has "? Ohje"). Menu screens show `MENU_HELP`, Rakenna shows `BUILD_HELP`, Sisusta `POHJA_HELP` (in a game it also lists that building's `Recipe.text` lines), Hirsityöt and hewing show the Rakennusohje, Sisätyöt and fastening the Sisätyöohje (`SISA`). `helpBack` is the screen to return to and picks the topic.
- Play screen layout (game, carve, fix; CSS in `styles.css`): the main target is tablets in landscape (1024–1400 px wide). At `min-width:900px` and `min-aspect-ratio:4/5` (not `orientation`, so a slightly tall desktop window still counts) it is a grid filling the viewport: bar on one row (long title gets "…"), picture left, a side column right with the progress (`.count`) above the hint, tray row at the bottom with a fixed minimum height. Nothing in it may resize when a hint, step or tray changes, or the picture jumps. Narrower screens use the stacked layout with the progress in the bar (`.cnt`); both copies are rendered and CSS hides one. When the tray overflows, a line under the hint says to swipe (wide layout only). Trays are one row: the pieces scroll sideways in an inner `.strip`, Peru/Valmis sits beside it (never over a piece). Check layout changes in a real browser at 1024×690 with "Suuri teksti" on.
- Drag: pointer events on the tray buttons; a drop snaps to the nearest available slot of the same kind within 80 SVG units. Tap: select a piece, tap the slot. A click right after a drag is ignored (`wasDrag`).

## Add a building
1. Add a `Building` in `src/game/buildings.ts` (reuse the kinds `log | door | window | roof | chimney | fire | pier | porch`; `lean: true` makes a single-slope roof) and put it in `BUILDINGS`.
2. A new piece kind needs: `PieceKind` in `types.ts`, a label and default colour in `buildings.ts`, and drawing in `Scene.tsx` (`Shape`, `Slot`, `ICON`).
3. A floor plan (Sisusta) instead: `mode: 'pohja'`, empty `targets`, and a `plan` whose rooms cover every cell exactly once, with `fixed`, `recipes`, `goal` and `pieces` (the recipes' pieces plus a few extras). Add a full furnishing that uses exactly `pieces` to the "every plan" test. A new furniture item needs `Item` in `types.ts`, `ITEM_LABEL` and `ITEM_SIZE` in `buildings.ts` and a drawing in `DRAW` in `Scene.tsx`.
4. Add a test for the new build order. Saves of a changed building reopen in build mode (unknown pieces dropped, other pieces kept), and saved buildings this version cannot read (unknown building, unknown phase) are kept untouched. Only change `KEY` in `storage.ts` if the save format itself changes, since that hides every existing save.

## Rules that must not regress
- All UI text is Finnish, short, one instruction at a time, verbs like "Valitse", "Napauta", "Maalaa". Never rely on colour alone (use labels, ＋ marks, ✓).
- Large targets (min 64 px), generous spacing, no precise dragging required. Tap-to-place must always work.
- Mistakes are never failures: wrong drops give a neutral hint, and "Peru" is always available while building.
- Everything focusable has an accessible name; keep keyboard support (Enter/Space on slots and buttons).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (there are no animations yet; add a setting only together with real motion). Sound and speech ("Puhe", reads the hint aloud with `speechSynthesis`) are off by default.
- Destructive actions (delete) need a second confirming tap.
- Playable offline and without localStorage. Do not cache external resources.
